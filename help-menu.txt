  - 🔎 search title Avatar

📊 Sort Search Results 
   - 🔠 By Title  → Alphabetical Order  
   - 📅 By Year   → Ascending Order  
   - ⭐ By Rating → Ascending Order  

  Format: sort {title, year, rating}
  ✅ Example: sort year
